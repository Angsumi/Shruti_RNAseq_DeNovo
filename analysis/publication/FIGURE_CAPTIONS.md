# Figure captions

**homology_review.png.** Numbers of protein and transcript records in exploratory Swiss-Prot homology review categories. Denominators are 41,281 predicted protein records and 318,934 transcript records; these are not read abundance, unique-gene counts or contamination proportions. Flags use coverage, identity, E-value and retained near-top reference-group agreement as described in the methods. Whole-body microbial associations and reference bias limit taxonomic interpretation.

**ampir_score_sensitivity.png.** Counts of records at or above several precursor-model score cutoffs for the entire predicted protein set and the original 370 vendor candidates. Scores are model outputs rather than validated activity measurements. Different thresholds give different reported candidate counts; cutoff 0.8 is exploratory.
